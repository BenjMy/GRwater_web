About

The GR-water project tackles the growing interest in understanding the fate of water in forest and post-fire management in the Spanish region.

💡 Why “GRwater”?
“GR” carries a dual meaning:
1️⃣ Groundwater, highlighting the project’s hydrogeological focus, and
2️⃣ Gran Recorrido, referring to a network of long-distance, well-managed hiking trails that cross landscapes — an analogy to tracing water pathways through forest ecosystems.

Open source

Our team works on several tools and projects, both developed in-house and across the larger scientific ecosystem:

  • pyTSEB: A python Two Source Energy Balance model for estimation of evapotranspiration with remote sensing data.
  • pyCATHY: A Python wrapper for CATHY (Catchment Hydrology) - [Camporese et al., 2010]
  • Centum: A Python package that facilitates water accounting through energy and water balance modeling
